Collaborative dataset produced within the TranscriboQuest 2026 annotation project. It gathers transcribed and segmented pages from seven medieval manuscripts, dated between the 9th and the 15th centuries, in Latin, Old/Middle French and Middle High German. In total, it comprises 1,316 annotated lines and 41,556 characters, with layout segmentation (main text zones, drop capitals, marginal text, numbering).

**My role:** annotator.
