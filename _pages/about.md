Kewei Wei is a Ph.D. candidate in early medieval history at Sorbonne Université, affiliated with Centre Roland Mousnier, Centre de Recherche en Histoire Médiévale, Moderne et Contemporaine (UMR 8596). Her research focuses on the Carolingian world in the ninth century. Her dissertation, *La Marche et la recomposition de l’ordre carolingien : Étude sur les Robertiens (853–898)*, will examine the Robertians and the role of the march in the reconfiguration of Carolingian political order during the second half of the century.

She is also developing an interest in digital humanities, particularly Handwritten Text Recognition (HTR). She works on the transcription of manuscripts written in Carolingian minuscule and on building datasets from them, with the aim of [e.g. contributing training data for HTR models.
